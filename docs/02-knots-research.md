## Not recommended (exclude from the teaching list)

- **Single overhand as a rappel stopper.** It can untie itself unless loaded.
  Use the double overhand instead.
- **Single figure-eight as a rappel stopper.** It may not stop the climber
  from rappelling off the end.
- **Clove hitch as the sole tie-in.** It can slip under some loads. It is not
  a tie-in knot on its own.
- **Double bowline as a tie-in.** Harder to inspect and tie correctly. Some
  sport climbers still use it, so this is a disagreement, not a settled ban.

## Disagreements

- **Figure-eight tie-in backup:** one source says a backup is needed, another
  says a 6-inch tail is enough. An AMGA reference cited on a forum says none is
  needed (unverified).
- **Rappel stopper:** double overhand is the most common recommendation.
  Others use the figure-eight on a bight or the overhand on a bight.
- **Bowline for sport tie-in:** older guides say avoid it; some sport climbers
  still use it.
- **Munter:** MTI notes it can twist the rope.
- **Locker in a figure-eight loop:** a forum claims the UIAA warns against
  clipping a locker into a tied figure-eight loop. Unverified.

**Not verified:** UIAA and AMGA technical pages could not be reached.
Every AMGA-attributed claim so far comes from a forum or a blog that cites
AMGA, not from AMGA itself.

**Safety claim to check:** one search result says several fatalities are
linked to using a figure-eight instead of a double overhand to join rappel
ropes. The claim came from a search summary and is not verified. It is listed
here because it bears on the stopper and joining decisions, not because it is
accepted.

### Disagreements to resolve before the teaching list is final

1. **Rappel stopper:** double overhand (research) or barrel knot (AMC list).
2. **Rope joining for rappel:** double overhand (research) or flat overhand
   (AMC list). Several sources treat these as different knots.
3. **Figure-eight on a bight:** needs a stopper per the climbing sources. The
   research table does not list this as a requirement.
